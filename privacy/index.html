Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

This website belongs to Marcus Piper Solutions LLC. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to information you share here.

What I collect

When you use a contact form, you send me your name, email address, the type of project, your message, and on the About page an optional phone number. I do not ask for payment details, passwords, or sensitive personal information through this site.

How I use it

I use what you send only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about. I never sell, rent, or trade your information, and I do not add you to a mailing list.

Services that help run this site

  • Formspree delivers contact form messages to my business email.
  • Google Workspace hosts that business email.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • GitHub provides the recent project activity shown on the home page, so your browser connects to GitHub to load it.
  • QR Server draws the QR code on the Digital Card page.

These companies handle information under their own privacy policies. This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics.

AI tools and your information

I use AI assistants, mainly Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, to help draft and test work faster. I make every decision and check the results myself. I do not put your private or sensitive information into these tools without your permission, and I will tell you which tools touch your project if you ask.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct, or delete anything you sent through this site. Email marcus@marcuspipersolutions.com and I will take care of it.
