My Workflow

How I Work

A real process, not a sales pitch. I use AI tools to draft and test faster. I make every decision, check every page, and never put your private information into them without your OK.

01

Discover

We talk through the actual problem you're solving for your customers, your team, or your operations.

02

Plan

I sketch the system: pages, screens, data, integrations. You approve the direction before any code is written.

03

See It Early

You get a working draft to react to early, not slides or mockups.

04

Test on Mobile

Every project is tested on real phones early. If it doesn't work in someone's hand, it doesn't ship.

05

Launch and Review

We launch, see how real people use it, and make the changes your plan includes.

06

Hand Off

You own the site and every account. Your team learns to update it, or I keep it running for you.
